          03 — THE DUPONT BREAKDOWN

          Your return on equity

          RETURN ON EQUITY—Net income ÷ average equity
          NET PROFIT MARGIN—Net income ÷ revenue
          ASSET TURNOVER—Revenue ÷ average assets
          EQUITY MULTIPLIER—Average assets ÷ average equity
          NET PROFIT MARGIN—× ASSET TURNOVER—× EQUITY MULTIPLIER—= —
          NET INCOME — REVENUE — AVERAGE ASSETS — AVERAGE EQUITY —

          ⓘ Three-step DuPont analysis is a financial snapshot, not investment, tax, or accounting advice. Review the mapped totals and consult a qualified professional for important decisions.

          A LITTLE CONTEXT

          Make the number useful.

          Break-even is a starting point for better questions, not a finish line.

          01

          What can you influence?

          Pricing, sourcing, and process improvements all change how much each sale contributes to covering overhead.

          02

          How reliable are the inputs?

          Use realistic costs and sales assumptions. Small changes can move the break-even point more than you expect.

          03

          What's your cushion?

          The margin of safety shows how much sales can fall before you reach break-even. More cushion means less risk.
